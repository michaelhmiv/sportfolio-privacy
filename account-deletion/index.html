Account control

Delete your Sportfolio account

How to request deletion of your Sportfolio account and its associated data. This workflow is permanent once processing completes.

How to request deletion

To request deletion of your Sportfolio account, email sportfolioholdings@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line “Sportfolio Account Deletion Help.”

Deletion requests must be initiated by the account holder so we can verify the account being removed. Once a request is verified and processed, the deletion is permanent.

Removed from active systems

  • Sportfolio account profile and sign-in access
  • Saved preferences, watchlists, schedules, and account-specific settings
  • Scouting, collection, and other active gameplay configuration tied to the account
  • Connected-application authorizations and account-linked continuation records

Records that may be retained

  • Trade, portfolio, reward, and action ledger records needed for fraud prevention, dispute handling, security review, or legal compliance
  • Operational logs and backups retained for a limited period while deletion is processed

Questions about account deletion: sportfolioholdings@gmail.com
